# Watch study — quality contract

Historical pilot contract: one reference watch, geometry/materials/dissection/explanation. The watch definition remains unchanged in this local twelve-object rollout. Public v1.1 is preserved.

## Identity and truth

A representative hand-wound teaching movement, Ø36.6 mm inside a Ø42 mm skeleton case. Not an ETA replica. The official ETA6497-2 dimensions (36.6 × 4.5 mm) establish a plausible scale only; its 3 Hz frequency is not used. This authored teaching train is 2.5 Hz / 18,000 beats per hour. Tooth tips, pallet contact surfaces and spring torque are illustrative; this is kinematic explanation, not a manufacturing or contact-force solver.

- Barrel 80 teeth : centre pinion10 → 8:1.
- Centre80 : third pinion10, third75 : fourth pinion10 → 60:1.
- Fourth80 : escape pinion8 → 10:1.
- Escape15, two half-tooth releases per tooth → 300 beats/min at one fourth-wheel revolution/minute.
- Motion work: cannon12 : minute36; coaxial minute pinion10 : hour40 → 12:1.
- Every angle, readout and counted beat reads the same pure watch model.
- Default display speed is one fifth physical time, clearly labelled. Slow multiplies this; neither changes the stated physical frequency.
- Power reserve is an explicit teaching parameter. Wind adds4h up to48h. No power stops the train. Reserve consumption is time-consistent; amplitude/torque are not claimed to be simulated.

## Geometry and hierarchy

Critical: recognisable case/crown/lugs; hollow case; plate and bridges with real holes; located arbors/jewel seats; rim/spokes/hub/pinion construction; actual gear pitch-centre spacing; distinct barrel, spring, ratchet and click; fork with two stones; balance, deforming hairspring, roller and stud; minute/hour/small-seconds display.

Detail inventory: stepped bezel, case lip, back ring, four lugs with drilled-looking bores, stem and knurled crown; screwed skeleton bridges, polished bevels, jewel chatons; pierced train wheels with tooth counts; countersunk/slotted fasteners; peripheral minute marks; motion-work wheels; barrel lid and spring attachment points; balance rim/spokes/timing screws, shock setting, regulator arm and curb pins.

White parts + thin charcoal visible outlines. Functional colours are semantic: orange energy; cyan train; pink escapement; blue oscillator; yellow display. Default accent coverage is restrained, selection/chapters expand it. UI remains white/black Manrope400/-4%, rectangular controls.

Dissection is staged by assembly. Outside parts lead; bridges clear before the train lifts. Inspection uses chapter-level reveal amounts and semantic framing, and Focus/Return restores previous view/separation. No global renderer replacement or changes to other models' materials.

## Acceptance

1. Macro and five viewing directions: coherent solid thickness, readable rims and cavities, clean silhouette; no decorative detail substituted for function.
2. Model: gear pitch distances and ratios, 12° releases, 2.5Hz/5beats, zero-power stop, winding/reserve bounds, hands12:1, finite poses and correct staged clearance.
3. Browser: desktop, tablet, phone and short viewport; no page scroll; reachable controls, pinned callout, Focus/Return, orbit/zoom, reduced motion and fallback.
4. Explain each of five systems in one short causal statement with a matching highlight and action. User can wind, run/pause, step one beat and remove power.

## Implementation notes

The mainplate is skeletonised; openings lighten the teaching view without a strength claim. The going train uses true pitch-centre distances and real wheel/pinion height planes. Winding spur gears are located geometrically, while their bevel interface and tooth contact faces remain schematic. The balance spring is a continuous ribbon with a fixed outer endpoint; spring deformation is a kinematic visual, not an elastic solution. Learning views explicitly hide covers/bridges and label that choice. The full dissection retains all parts.

Validated 124 named features, including integral marks/knurls; they are not 124 separate service components. Default one fifth time = one displayed beat per wall-clock second. W/B operate winding/one beat. This paragraph records the original pilot; current gallery scope is documented below.
