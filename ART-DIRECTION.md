# Final art-direction review

The twelve objects retain the approved drawing language: four white surface tones, thin visible edges and labelled functional colours. The full object owns the opening screen; Learn opens on request. A selected chapter frames its mechanism, and full dissection restores every component.

| Review area | Result |
|---|---|
| Hero composition | All twelve enter without an inspector. Overview, chapter and exploded views were reviewed in the actual renderer. |
| Colour | Structural context stays white. Functional selection and the current chapter share a colour and a text label. |
| Geometry | Existing reference definitions preserved. Nine-object bores, seats, pins, guides and parking clearances pass their numerical checks. Enlarged HDD guide tracks now have an additional 0.1 mm offset above the platter. |
| Inspection | Pinned callouts keep Focus reachable; Return restores the camera, separation and chapter. |
| Small screens | Four viewport sizes, held phases, Playback/Settings and touch controls checked without page/panel scrolling. |
| Distribution | Thirteen deterministic standalone entries, dist parity, per-object metadata and sitemap. Clean thumbnails and a new 1200 × 630 share cover. |
| Reproduction | Pinned Node dependencies and portable Playwright browser checks; no workstation paths required. |

The models are explanatory kinematics. Their sources and specific simplifications remain in PRESETS.md, GALLERY-V2.md and WATCH-STANDARD.md. No general collision/contact-force, optical or acoustic solver is claimed.

This is a release candidate. Production replacement and the final GitHub release remain a separate approval step; no social distribution is included.
